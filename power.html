# Reset Random

Keep playing Retry

How to play: Use the left and right arrow keys to rotate the board. Tiles fall downward after each rotation, and equal tiles merge into one. No new tiles appear. Solve the puzzle by merging every tile into one.

Note: Vertical keys and swipes are not used. A puzzle ends when only one tile remains or no future merge is possible.
